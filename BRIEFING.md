## Style
Loves: huge elaborate movie-set scenes, outrageous pranks, funny disasters, interactive games and prizes, food/gross-out humor, gaming action scenes, family participation, reusable props, dramatic lighting, prep-ahead big scenes.
Don'ts: generic ideas, elf just sitting, toilet-paper pranks, repackaged rejects, too many similar games, forced participation for Lia, nightly construction, more horror right now, unnecessary supplies while brainstorming, cluttered designs.
A good idea delivers at least one (ideally 2+): huge visual surprise, genuinely funny prank, exciting interactive activity, special gift/prize, personalized moment, memorable tradition. Bold but physically safe (stairs, suspended objects, lights, large structures).
